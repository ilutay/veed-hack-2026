# Art Direction

Default style until the product direction is defined:

- clean educational editorial illustration
- concrete diagrams and metaphors over decorative backgrounds
- consistent color palette across slides
- no tiny labels or paragraphs inside generated images
- leave room for webpage-rendered title and caption overlays

Each prompt should include:

- slide id
- slide title
- visual brief
- key concept to emphasize
- shared style block
- negative guidance for text-heavy or misleading imagery
